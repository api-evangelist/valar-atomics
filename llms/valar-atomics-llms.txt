# Valar Atomics

> Valar Atomics is a nuclear energy company developing high-temperature gas reactors (HTGR) with TRISO fuel, graphite moderators, and helium coolant. It builds grid-independent "gigasites" of many small reactors to power heavy industry — hydrogen production via the sulfur-iodine cycle, AI data-center power, industrial process heat (steel, bauxite electrolysis), and clean synthetic hydrocarbon fuels via Fischer-Tropsch with captured CO2. Its Ward 250 test reactor is a partnership with the U.S. Department of Energy, the San Rafael Energy Research Center, and the State of Utah. Valar Atomics publishes no public API, developer portal, or SDKs.
